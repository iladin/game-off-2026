## Jam Critic

Third review. Trends are against R0012. Evidence:
- Fresh shots this run with `./scripts/shots.sh <slug> 300 1200` (start, 300 and 1200 frames) for all 8 games, plus `./scripts/shots.sh hub` (0 of 8 bits save). I looked at play-1200 for all 8, play-300 for Red Shift, Phase Shift, Cargo Shift and Night Watch, and the hub.
- Existing gauntlet shots from after T-0030/T-0043 (`.tools/shots/gauntlet/card2.png`, `clear100.png`, `qa-w120.png`, 19:53 to 20:33). I did not re-run the gauntlet.
- A grep of every `Synth.*` call in `games/*/*.gd`, `shared/arcade.gd` and `hub/hub.gd`. I also read the Red Shift spawn code (`games/redshift/rules.gd` `_spawn`, `spawn_bands`) and the Phase Shift rules constants.

I couldn't hear anything, so audio is scored from the code.

### Collection

- **Overall (7.4):** It is still ONE entry with a point of view, and the R0013 P1 batch closed most of the visual gap in the second half. Phase Shift has a skyline, Cargo Shift has a solid hull, and the gauntlet has a ceremony. What holds it under 8 is that three games still look like a tech demo in a still frame: Red Shift spawns one enemy at a time, Night Watch's big view is an empty corridor, and Cargo Shift barely moves when you play well. The R0013 to R0017 runs went into popup placement, which voters won't notice.
- **Theme (8.6):** The register now runs all the way through. The hub's eight sense words, the gauntlet card that flips each slot from 0 to 1 under the next sense word (card2), and the clear card with eight gold 1s and OVERFLOW (clear100) make "one word read eight ways inside a literal shift register" visible from the first screen to the last. Every game and the hub play `shift` on their verb.
- Graphics stay disciplined: bible palette, TRACE grid, the same HUD stack, clear corners and no glyph boxes in any of the 9 fresh shots. Audio is unchanged. Shapeshifter has the thinnest sound (1 `Synth.tone`). Cargo Shift and Night Watch have the richest (4 each). The gauntlet adds `shift` and `coin` on its card and register, but it has no voice of its own.

**Shapeshifter (slot 1, SHAPE)**
- *Best:* still the cleanest first 10 seconds in the set: one verb, three big shape buttons, and the selected one outlined in pink (play-1200).
- *Worst:* R0012 fix 8 was never filed. In the gauntlet shot (qa-w120) the hole is still a big soft pink halo of stacked 1.3x/1.6x circles around the player, which is not the hard-edged look the bible asks for. The audio is presets plus one tone, so the three shapes all sound the same.
- *Ship bar:* **meets** (7.2).

**Gear Grind (slot 2, GEAR)**
- *Best:* the race now reads. The start is decluttered (T-0045, T-0057), the `YOU LEAD 78 m` bracket sits clear of the car, TOP shows inside the dial, and the skyline and posts add depth (play-1200). The rpm-following engine tone is still the best custom audio.
- *Worst:* Innovation (5.8) is the lowest in the set. It is a well-made one-button drag racer that voters have seen before. The remaining open items (T-0061, T-0062) are pixel nits.
- *Ship bar:* **meets** (7.2).

**Upper Case (slot 3, KEY)**
- *Best:* the most literal SHIFT. Two big drawn SHIFT keys with F and J labelled in their corners, the keyboard strip lighting the letter, and the PERFECT feedback (play-1200).
- *Worst:* T-0031 has been blocked since R0014 (no Chromium on the machine). Nobody knows whether the web build tells Left Shift from Right Shift, and the web build is the one that gets judged. The F/J fallback is labelled, so the worst case is "press F or J", which still weakens the game's whole joke.
- *Ship bar:* **meets** (7.0).

**Bit Catcher (slot 4, BIT)**
- *Best:* the BYTE banner, the GOOD progress squares and the lime-lit sockets make a completed byte feel like an event (play-1200).
- *Worst:* unchanged since R0012. There are three rows of squares in the top 190 px (lives, byte progress, target), and the non-socket target cells are faint PANEL blocks. A first-time voter still needs about 5 s to see that the top row is the byte you need. The register rotation has no tone of its own.
- *Ship bar:* **meets** (6.9).

**Phase Shift (slot 5, PHASE)**
- *Best:* T-0034 worked. The two parallax skyline rows (filled cyan in the current world, violet outlines for the other) fill the y 200 to 520 band, so a still frame now says "two worlds" at a glance (play-300, play-1200).
- *Worst:* the play itself is still flat. The ground is a single strip at y 560 to 600 with no height changes, so a jump is only ever a gap jump, and the orbs are small DIM outline circles that are hard to see against the skyline. It is still the most familiar genre (Innovation 5.6).
- *Ship bar:* **meets** (6.9). It is no longer the weakest.

**Cargo Shift (slot 6, CARGO)**
- *Best:* the hull is solid with a teal waterline that follows the sea (T-0036, T-0042, T-0047), the crates are apart, and the RODE IT banner plus the balance meter are clear. The storm audio is still the richest in the set.
- *Worst:* when you play well, nothing moves. play-300 and play-1200 are almost the same frame: the ship is level, the two crates are in the same spots, and the needle is centred. The cargo never visibly shifts unless you are failing, so a voter watching a good run sees a still picture.
- *Ship bar:* **meets** (6.9).

**Night Watch (slot 7, NIGHT)**
- *Best:* the idea and the sound. Creeper eyes in the thumbnails, a loop that thins out as danger rises, static tones, and the 12 to 6 AM clock (play-1200 shows 2 AM).
- *Worst:* the big view, 730x450 px and the largest thing on screen, is still an empty corridor with a white dash a few px wide at the end, in both play-300 and play-1200. T-0035's danger bar only appears at d > 0.6, so in normal play the main frame shows nothing. The game is played in the 300 px thumbnails.
- *Ship bar:* **meets** (6.9).

**Red Shift (slot 8, RED)**
- *Best:* the Doppler reading. The hue chips, the BRAKE and BOOST pads and the recoloured ship, bullets and enemies all switch together, the loop transposes per band, and two-finger touch now works (T-0033).
- *Worst:* R0012's top fix was never filed. `_spawn()` still releases ONE enemy per `spawn_gap` (`games/redshift/rules.gd:202`), and no enemy fires. play-300 shows one live enemy. play-1200 shows three in a column only because the gap has shrunk, plus two hollow other-band squares at the edge. For the slot-8 spectacle closer it is still the thinnest screen in the set, and five of the last six Red Shift tasks went into popups.
- *Ship bar:* **meets, just** (6.6, Graphics 6.5). It is the weakest game.

**OVERFLOW (gauntlet)**
- *Best:* it has the ceremony now. Each game gets a card with the sense word at about 90 px in its accent over the 8-bit register with the current slot flipped to 1 (card2). The clear card shows eight gold 1s under OVERFLOW (clear100), and the GAME n OF 8 / TIME / RUN LIVES gutter HUD is clean (qa-w120).
- *Worst:* the clear card sits on pure black, not BG, and it says OVERFLOW twice (the title and `OVERFLOW: 1234`). The run has no audio identity of its own: there is no loop that ties eight games into one run, and no rising tone as the bits fill.
- *Ship bar:* **meets** (7.5).

**Hub**
- *Best:* still the cover image and the theme statement in one frame: eight cells with sense words and accent bars, the read head, the bus dots and the 9th OVERFLOW cell.
- *Worst:* nothing moves until you press something. VISION section 7 polish item 3 (the register rotates under a fixed head, as a literal circular shift, plus idle attract) is unbuilt. The footer still leads with keyboard words (`LEFT / RIGHT choose ...`). With 0 bits set, the OVERFLOW teaser (T-0028) is still undecided.
- *Ship bar:* n/a (7.6).
