### Collection

| 7.2 (+0.6) | 6.7 (+0.2) | 7.1 (+0.1) | 6.9 (+0.6) | 7.4 (+1.0) | 8.4 (+0.6) |

- **Overall (7.2):** It now reads as ONE finished entry with a point of view. All 8 bits are set, and the hub row SHAPE / GEAR / KEY / BIT / PHASE / CARGO / NIGHT / RED with a gold OVERFLOW cell at the end is the best screenshot in the build: a voter gets the whole pitch in two seconds. What still holds it under 8 is that the second half of the slate (Phase Shift, Cargo Shift, Red Shift) looks thinner and emptier than the first four. A voter who plays the games in slot order feels the quality drop just as the collection should be building to its spectacle closer.
- **Theme (8.4):** One word read eight ways, inside a hub that is literally a shift register, and now a finale that remixes the register: that is a clear, clever and cohesive reading. Every game plays `Synth.sfx("shift")` on its one verb (checked in code for all 8), so the theme can be heard as well as seen.
- Graphics stay disciplined: palette, TRACE grid, the same HUD stack and the same corners everywhere, and no glyph boxes in any shot. Audio rose the most among the scored categories: all 8 games now have custom `Synth.tone` voices. Examples: the Cargo Shift storm noise, the Night Watch static and thinning loop, the Upper Case clack and ding, and loops that transpose on the verb in Phase Shift and Red Shift. `hit` is now used in Night Watch and Red Shift.

**Shapeshifter (slot 1, SHAPE)**
- *Best:* still the strongest first impression. It's one verb, three big shape buttons, and the selected button is outlined in pink (play-1200).
- *Worst:* the halo around the hole in play-1200. The 1.3x/1.6x glow of the circle hole plus the triangle player merges into a lumpy, soft-edged blob. That breaks the "flat, hard-edged" shape language, and at impact time the wall is still a flat PANEL slab from x 153 to 1127. Innovation (6.0) is the lowest of the first four games.
- *Ship bar:* **meets** (7.2).

**Gear Grind (slot 2, GEAR)**
- *Best:* T-0014 worked. The skyline, the roadside posts and the rival gap bracket make it look like a race now, and the engine tone that follows rpm is still the best custom audio in the set.
- *Worst:* the start of each heat is cluttered (play-1500). The staging light tree sits on top of the rival car, and `RIVAL AHEAD 8 m` in BAD is drawn over the checkered start line and the light pole. Three overlapping things, in the one moment the player has to read GO.
- *Ship bar:* **meets** (7.0).

**Upper Case (slot 3, KEY)**
- *Best:* the glow fix and the `LET LOWER PASS` ramp banner on its own panel (r11-1297). The hinted Shift is now unmistakable, and the clack and ding give it its own sound.
- *Worst:* the risk, not the look. Left/Right `InputEventKey.location` in Chromium is still UNVERIFIED (T-0031). If the web build can't tell the two Shift keys apart, the most literal game in the collection quietly becomes "press F or J".
- *Ship bar:* **meets** (7.0).

**Bit Catcher (slot 4, BIT)**
- *Best:* the turnaround of the review. Lit lime `1`s in the register, a target row above it, a lane line from the falling bit to its cell, and GOOD progress squares (play-1200). The state can be read at a glance now.
- *Worst:* the screen is busy and only partly explained. There are two rows of squares under the score (lives plus the byte progress), then a target row whose non-socket cells are faint PANEL squares with thin lines down to the register. It takes about 5 s of study to see that the top row is "the byte you need". Audio uses presets plus two tones, and no tone marks the register rotation itself.
- *Ship bar:* **meets** (6.9, up from 6.3). The R0006 cut watch is closed.

**Phase Shift (slot 5, PHASE), first review**
- *Best:* the core read is instant. The floor is violet or cyan, the cells of the other world are hollow outlines, and the player square and the phase button both show the current world (play-300, play-1200). The loop restarts transposed on each phase (`R.loop_notes(body.world)`), which is the best "the music shifts too" moment in the set.
- *Worst:* about two thirds of the screen is empty. Everything happens on one strip at y 560 to 600, with flat ground, no height changes and nothing above y 500 except orbs and popups. In a still frame it looks like a prototype next to Gear Grind or Night Watch. It is also the most familiar genre in the set (Innovation 5.6).
- *Ship bar:* **meets**, just (6.6, Graphics 6.0).

**Cargo Shift (slot 6, CARGO), first review**
- *Best:* the storm. Three noise tones (wave hit, swell and crate thud), a 92 bpm sine loop, rain streaks, and a teal swell warning shape on the horizon (play-1950). The balance meter at the top is clear.
- *Worst:* when you play well, nothing moves. In play-300 and play-1200 the bot holds the ship level and the frames are almost identical. The hull is an unfilled INK wireframe that the sea shows through, and in the storm two crates overlap each other (play-1950). The verb is "hold a direction gently", which makes for a calm first 20 s that a voter may read as "nothing is happening".
- *Ship bar:* **meets** (6.7).

**Night Watch (slot 7, NIGHT), first review**
- *Best:* the idea and the sound. The watched feed freezes, the unwatched ones creep closer, and thumbnails go TRACE to BAD with distance (`watch_draw.gd:184`). The loop thins from calm to sparse as danger grows (`LOOP_CALM`/`LOOP_THIN`), and there are static and thump tones. This uses silence as information, as VISION asked. It is the most innovative single game in the set (7.6).
- *Worst:* the big view, 730x450 px and the largest thing on screen, is an empty corridor most of the time (start, play-1200). The threat in it is a white dash a few px wide, and the real game is played in the 300 px thumbnails on the right. The visual energy is in the wrong place.
- *Ship bar:* **meets** (6.8).

**Red Shift (slot 8, RED), first review**
- *Best:* the Doppler reading is clever and shown well. The three hue chips at the top left, the BRAKE and BOOST pads with their colour strips, and the ship, bullets and enemies all recoloured to the current band (play-300, play-1200). The loop transposes per band, and `hit` and `coin` both fire on a kill.
- *Worst:* it is sparse for the spectacle closer. `GAP_START` is 1.6 s and `SPEED_START` is 150 px/s, so there is usually ONE enemy on screen (both play shots), and enemies don't shoot. A shmup with a single target at a time reads as a tech demo.
- *Ship bar:* **meets, on the line** (6.5, Graphics 6.4).

**OVERFLOW (gauntlet), first review**
- *Best:* the Innovation pitch delivered. Eight games back to back at `difficulty = 1`, 8 s each, 3 run lives, with a clean gutter HUD (`GAME 3 OF 8 / TIME 4.5 / RUN LIVES`, t27/gauntlet-1200). It is the moment that turns "a collection" into "an entry".
- *Worst:* it has no ceremony of its own. A game change is a hard cut, T-0030 (the timer runs during each game's intro) eats part of the 8 s, and the end is the generic result card (`CLEAR!`, `OVERFLOW: 135`, gauntlet/result) with no register moment: no carry rippling through the 8 bits, no gold flash. No custom audio either; it reuses each game's sounds.
- *Ship bar:* **meets** (7.0).

**Hub**
- *Best:* the eight lit `1`s with their sense words, the accent bars and the gold OVERFLOW cell. It is the cover image and the theme statement in one frame.
- *Worst:* in QA's save state the info panel shows `NEW` next to `CLEARED`, which contradicts itself (probably a test save; check it in real play). The footer still leads with keyboard words.
- *Ship bar:* n/a (the hub is 7.6 overall).
