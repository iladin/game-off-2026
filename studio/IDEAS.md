# Ideas: framings and pitches for SHIFT

The Creative Director draws the slate from here at kickoff (or invents better). Anyone can add lines; Daniel's additions go first in line.

## Daniel's decisions (2026-11-02)
- One jam entry: a collection of small games about SHIFT behind one hub ("like godot-web-games, but better").
- Framing: **the team decides** (Creative Director, at kickoff).
- Controls: **free per game**. Keyboard AND touch are still required in every game.

## Framings (pick one, or beat them)
- **A. SHIFT REGISTER.** The hub is an 8-bit register; each bit is one game, and clearing a game sets its bit. Navigation shifts the register left and right. At 0xFF the finale **OVERFLOW** unlocks: every game remixed as a fast gauntlet of 6-second bursts. Clean, nerdy visual identity (glowing bits, bus lines), and it reads "shift bits" literally.
- **B. GRAVEYARD SHIFT.** One night shift, 10 pm to 6 am, at a strange 24-hour facility. Each hour is a different job (a game); short coworker banter between hours; dawn is the ending. Narrative glue and mood, and it reads "work shift".
- **C. GEARBOX.** Each game is a gear (1 to 6, plus R). Higher gears are harder remixes of the same games; R plays a game's rules in reverse. Reads "shift gears"; thinner on story.

## Pitches (genre · how it reads SHIFT)
1. **Upper Case** (rhythm) · capital letters scroll past the cursor; hit the right Shift on each capital, using the touch-typist's rule (opposite hand from the letter). Touch: left/right halves.
2. **Phase Shift** (auto-runner) · two overlapping worlds, each platform exists in only one; jump and phase between realities.
3. **Gear Grind** (timing racer) · shift up at the tach's sweet spot, downshift for corners; missed shifts stall.
4. **Red Shift** (shmup) · your speed sets which hues you can see; brake to blueshift, boost to redshift; only enemies in your visible colour can be hit.
5. **Cargo Shift** (balance) · keep a container ship level in a storm by moving ballast while cargo slides with every wave.
6. **Turnabout** (gravity puzzle) · rotate the whole maze 90° and let gravity carry the ball to the exit.
7. **Graveyard Shift** (lane defense) · a gravedigger working two lanes at night; swing the shovel at whatever rises.
8. **Shapeshifter** (reflex) · cycle circle/square/triangle to fit through the walls rushing at you.

## Bench
- **Night Watch** (attention) · two camera feeds; things only move while you're not watching.
- **Swing Shift** (momentum) · trapeze artist: release and grab to swing bar to bar.
- **Shifting Sands** (physics puzzle) · tilt a falling-sand world to fill the jars before the hourglass empties.
- **Pitch Shift** (music) · steer a voice up and down the scale to sing along with the melody.
- **Overtime** (sorting) · stamp and pack on a factory line; at every shift change the controls swap.
- **Shifting Shadows** (stealth) · a vampire stays in the shade as the sun crosses the sky and the shadows swing.
- **Bit Catcher** (catcher/puzzle) · bits fall into an 8-wide register; shift it left/right to build the target byte.
- **Tectonic** (strategy-lite) · nudge plates to keep cities on stable ground as quakes roll through.
